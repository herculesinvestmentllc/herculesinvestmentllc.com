About Hercules

An operating company, not a holding on paper.

Hercules Investments LLC is a US-registered e-commerce group. We run the whole chain in-house — product research, sourcing, listing, advertising, fulfilment — with a single seven-person team and our own warehouse.

◆ Founded 2016 · Pennsauken, New Jersey

We started the way most sellers do — sourcing other people's products. It taught us the one thing that actually compounds in this business: owning the brand, the listing and the customer relationship.

Today every product we launch starts as data, not a hunch. If the margin, the competition and the demand curve don't clear our thresholds, we don't buy the first carton.

2016

Hercules Investments LLC is registered in New Jersey on 29 April 2016.

Our Vision

To become a group of consumer brands that customers re-order from by name — not by whichever listing happened to rank first that day.

Our Mission

To decide with data, protect margin at every step, and ship every order from a chain we own end to end.
